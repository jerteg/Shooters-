How the numbers are calculated

Per-90 figures are totals divided by minutes, times 90. Shot and on-target counts are rebuilt from shots per 90, on-target % and minutes, so they can be off by one.

Combined adds both seasons together (shots, shots on target and minutes) and only includes players with data in both. Because last season has many more minutes, it weighs heavier in the combined figures early in the season.

Fair odds come from a Poisson distribution with the player's per-90 rate over 90 minutes (with Wissel+ his stats carry over to the substitute, so the full match counts) or over his average minutes per match. Fair odds = 1 ÷ chance. They contain no bookmaker margin, so a bookmaker price only looks interesting when it's clearly above them. Real shot counts have slightly more zeros than Poisson assumes, so the fair odds for 1+ are a little optimistic.

Players are listed at their current club. Players who have since left for a club outside the league are left out.
